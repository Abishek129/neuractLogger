**Evidence:** /home/rohith/backend_LOS/LoggerFast/agent/rust_jobs/src/main.rs:677 in the WORKING TREE still reads `let jobs = load_running_jobs(meta_client.as_ref()).await?;` directly inside the supervisor `loop {` at line 676.
